What most people don't know

Your low property tax can move with you.

Since 2021, Prop 19 has let California homeowners 55 and older take their tax base to a new home anywhere in the state. Up to three times.

Yearly tax on a $1.1M home
Without Prop 19about $12,100
With Prop 19about $5,000
About $7,100 a year stays with you.

Example from the guide: a home bought in 1999, assessed at about $450,000 today, at a 1.1% tax rate. Your numbers will vary. The guide walks through who qualifies, the price limits, and the timing.
